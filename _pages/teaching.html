Teaching

I have taught and assisted several undergraduate and postgraduate courses, focusing on software engineering, formal methods, and human–computer interaction.

University of Lahore, Islamabad, Pakistan (2019–2021)

  • Software Engineering — Explores the basics of Software Engineering and framwork for developing a softwares and different software process models
  • Human Computer Interaction — Explores principles and methods of designing user-centered interfaces, focusing on usability, interaction design, and evaluation of interactive systems
  • Software Requirements and Specification — Covers techniques for eliciting, documenting, and managing functional and non-functional requirements to ensure high-quality software specifications
  • Discrete Structures — Provides the mathematical foundations for computer science, including logic, sets, relations, graphs, and combinatorics, to support problem solving and system modeling

IQRA National University, Peshawar, Pakistan (2017–2019)

  • Formal Methods in Software Engineering — Introduces mathematical techniques and specification languages for modeling, verifying, and validating software systems
  • Enterprise Systems Enigneering — Provides an overview of enterprise resource planning and large-scale business systems, focusing on integration and implementation concepts
